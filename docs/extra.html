From a Homegrown Brand to a Household Name

Savera Pipes Group of Industries has a rather grounding history, beginning with the family starting from two machines, producing garden hoses. We have now expanded to three plants producing piping and electrical wiring solutions. The year 1992 saw the emergence of the Savera Group of Industries which was founded by Mr. B.R. Giria and his elder son then joined Mr. Vinod Giria. Several years later Mr. Vineet Giria, a '97 MBA Graduate, subsequently became part of Savera Group of Industries and over the years Savera Group of Industries expanded significantly as it turned into a big multi-product, multi-brand company. In the event that you are searching for the 'best pvc pipe manufacturers near me', Savera Pipes are among the top ten pvc pipe companies in the India.

Corporate Philosophy Block

Through reputable brands such as Champion, Champion Flex, and Champion Gold, Savera Group of Industries has gained the confidence of its customers by delivering high-quality products, competitive pricing, and effective after-sales support. The company has established a solid reputation in the agricultural, industrial, and domestic markets both in India and internationally.

Our values

We prioritize quality excellence, ensuring superior products and innovation through advanced technology. Integrity, reliability, and customer satisfaction drive us, offering tailored solutions with timely delivery and consistency.

Vision

To be the leading producer and supplier of high-quality, durable, and aesthetically designed PVC pipes, expanding our presence across India and globally while setting new benchmarks in innovation, reliability, and customer satisfaction.

Mission

To maintain our brand value by consistently manufacturing and supplying superior PVC pipes, ensuring product excellence through stringent quality control, advanced technology, and customized solutions, while fulfilling bulk orders efficiently and meeting customer needs with precision.
